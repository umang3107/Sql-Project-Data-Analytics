# Business Insights

## Sales Insights
- Technology category generated the highest sales revenue.
- West region contributed the highest overall sales.
- A small number of customers contributed a large share of revenue.

## Profitability Insights
- Several products generated negative profit despite strong sales.
- Furniture category showed lower profitability compared to Technology.
- Higher discounts appeared to reduce profit margins.

## Customer Insights
- Premium customers generated significant revenue contribution.
- Customer purchasing behavior varied across regions.

## Regional Insights
- Certain states performed significantly better in terms of profit.
- Regional sales performance was uneven across the business.
